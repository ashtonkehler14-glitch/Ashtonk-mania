# More of Neru's poses

Cut out of her song's music video with `tools/neru-art.py` like the ones in `public/`, but not shipped: each screen
shows one pose of its own (`NeruCameo.POSE`), and her easter eggs a few more (`NeruEggs.EGG_POSES`). To use one, move
it into `public/`, give it a screen in `POSE` (or an easter egg) and its head size in `NeruCameo.HEAD`:

| pose | head size |
|---|---|
| pointat | 0.32 |
| side | 0.37 |
| smirk | 0.53 |
| snicker | 0.21 |
